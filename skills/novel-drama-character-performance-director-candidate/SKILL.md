# Character Performance Director Candidate

This isolated overlay owns `PERFORMANCE_INTENT` proposals only. It does not replace the active Skill or change Canon, identity, wardrobe, topology, camera, model, or execution authorization.

Use the approved master identity and the current shot's objective, obstacle and tactic. Select the few acting instructions that make the event readable rather than pasting a whole performance profile into every prompt.

For a reaction, connect the visible trigger to onset, hold and release where relevant. Distinguish an expression that signals awareness from evidence that the actor noticed a specific cause; temporal ordering alone may leave the causal interpretation unverified.

For assistance, describe the helper's visible effort and the partner's response, keeping contact ownership and release consistent with the approved motion plan. A maintained handhold does not by itself prove material pulling. Refer support/force ambiguity back to cinematic and spatial owners instead of inventing hidden strength or geometry.

Preserve approved natural facial texture, stable asymmetries, marks and imperfections. Do not smooth these away as a prompt cleanup or vary stable identity features to imply emotion. Expression detail is a proposal, not a diagnosis of an internal state from an image.

Use existing `beats`, `reaction_cue`, `body_state`, `eye_life`, `breath` and `business` fields; keep evidence annotations in a sidecar. Preserve the master voice identity, but leave the required `voice_instruction` empty for an approved silent shot. Hand the intent to the optimizer's existing HEP/compiler path; do not claim that successful text compilation proves performance quality.
