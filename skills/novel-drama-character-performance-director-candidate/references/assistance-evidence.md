# Assistance evidence

Keep performance proposals in the existing acting fields. Use the candidate `qa_feedback.py` force-chain sidecar for review evidence only: initiating source, existing contact, partner response, current support and next support. Refer unknown topology or support geometry to the spatial/director owners rather than inventing it.

For every component distinguish an approved design requirement, observed image evidence and an unverified physical interpretation. A maintained grip alone does not prove active assistance. A reacting body does not identify the force source. Palm contact does not establish forearm contact, load share or load-transfer time. Earlier screen contact does not prove earlier weight acceptance. For a synthetic comparison, “手掌到墙冠较早” describes earlier palm contact only; an actual-media finding also requires actual PTS and the contact-only qualification.

Keep onset and response evidence tied to the actual video. Never substitute a design deadline for an observed time or treat a coincident response as proven causality. If an actor's support is hidden, record the gap; do not infer zero load or a successful handover. Check new acting business against occupied hands and existing support without adding another touch, push or recovery beat.

The sidecar cannot turn missing force evidence into PASS, change a historical QA result, or grant a retry. Preserve stable facial texture, marks, asymmetry and the approved silent-shot policy. Improvements in expression or wording do not authorize camera changes, model calls or new generation.
