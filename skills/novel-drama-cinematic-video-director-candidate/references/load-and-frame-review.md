# Motion load and frame review

For a difficult shot, the existing director remains the owner of timing, camera and action intent. Add a compact previs/shot-review sidecar from the approved plan and supplied media evidence; do not build a replacement timeline or change the intent schema.

Count independent active events separately from maintained grip, passive support, cloth movement and camera motion. Record actor, event, design interval and required attention. A suggested action-count threshold is a configurable `WARN`, not a provider capability limit or a reason to reject a valid parallel action. The candidate `qa_feedback.py` motion-load review supplements the accepted plan; it does not decide a new duration or discard required beats.

Cross-frame assertions need original frame indexes, actual PTS, evidence scope and a specific claim. Keep the last visible and first absent frames adjacent when diagnosing a disappearance. Sparse samples cannot certify the intervening sequence. A first visible spike is not proof of world-space birth; continued foot contact is not foot release. A supported camera-exit claim requires the spatial owner's evidence, not a label added to the assertion.

Keep separate results for text alignment, visible motion and physical interpretation. Grip may be visible while material assistance remains unverified. Preserve every historical hard-QA result, including prop failure even when the character/camera seam passes. In a synthetic comparison, “手掌到墙冠较早” denotes earlier palm contact, not earlier forearm loading. Each compared critical frame pair must retain its own evidence interval.

A sidecar PASS describes only its checked proposition and inputs. Do not promote it to whole-shot QA, amend Canon, shorten a failed interval, make a derivative endpoint, submit a model call or initiate an automatic repair.
